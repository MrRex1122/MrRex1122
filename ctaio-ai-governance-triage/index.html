AI Governance Triage

A 60-second decision aid for CTOs and CAIOs: answer six questions and get a lightweight governance priority plus the next controls to consider.

1. Will the system handle confidential, personal, or customer data?
2. Can the AI trigger external actions (send, publish, approve, purchase, modify records)?
3. Does the output affect hiring, finance, access, safety, or another high-impact decision?
4. Is a human required to review the AI output before a consequential action?
5. Can you trace inputs, model/version, output, and final action in logs?
6. Is there a defined owner who can pause or disable the workflow?
Triage only — not legal advice
